## Matched conventional workflow

`baseline.py` runs independently of graph analysis. Shared input authentication is
intentionally identical: evaluator receipts, developer configuration/change receipts,
enterprise acceptance/scope receipts, trust pins, dependencies and authorization
rules. Ordinary structured files store dependencies, audit digests and receiver-signed
review tickets. No baseline capability is intentionally disabled. In this bounded
model each listed basis is independently sufficient; more complicated conjunctive
workflows are outside both implementations' comparison.

Both systems require the same three dependency rows in the complete case, two
completeness declarations and the same receiver event-scope judgment. The flat
baseline scans three dependency rows for a complete case, two for the missing case.
There is no measured capture-effort reduction. Shared verification is reusable;
OpenLine adds source/claim/relation construction, policy binding, graph signing and
impact replay. The baseline needs only the small flat-table evaluation and status
record. On these two decisions, the baseline is easier to integrate. This is a
structural observation, not a controlled maintainability study.

`comparison.json` records machine nanoseconds for the flat evaluation and graph
build/sign/replay pipeline. Those workloads differ and are explicitly labeled;
they are not evidence of comparative algorithm speed or human effort. Dependency
capture and review time are NOT MEASURED. No reviewers, customer, independent
adopter, paid API, or business workflow participated. Both can transfer signed JSON
and replay checks; OpenLine additionally supplies its existing graph witness report.
A flat dependency table can also produce witnesses without a new governance platform.

## Upstream capture assumption

The event and mappings are hand-authored together. No prospective capture reliability
has been earned. Missing or uncertain declarations cause review, but wrongly claiming
completeness with an irrelevant alternative preserves payments incorrectly in both
systems. The adversarial suite records this known bypass rather than hiding it.
A next empirical study would capture manifests before changes are selected, obtain
role-separated gold, and measure corrections, omitted material dependencies and
capture/reconstruction time. It must be a separate study; the existing frozen
Decision Recall instruments are unchanged.
