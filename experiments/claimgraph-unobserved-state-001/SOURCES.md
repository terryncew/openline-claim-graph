## DNative-Twin

Junjie Pang, Zhenzhen Xie, Haoke Han, Ying He, Jing Wang, Gang Liu. “DNative-Twin: Decision Graphs and Digital Twins for Reconstructable Agentic Decisions.” arXiv:2609.03787v1. Submitted September 3, 2026.

Relevant reported result: in a 300-instance controlled experiment, unresolved-divergence recall was 0 from graph structure alone, 0.667 with replay-contract state, and 1.0 with verification results.

Canonical locator: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03787`
