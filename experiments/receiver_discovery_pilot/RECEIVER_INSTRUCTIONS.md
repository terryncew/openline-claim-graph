# Receiver Instructions v0.3

You are reviewing a packet of source material with one accompanying aid.

Your task is to decide whether the sources contain one consequential fault line that a later decision-maker should notice. A fault line is either:

- two claims that cannot both guide the same decision without resolving their tension; or
- an unstated premise required to move from one stated claim to another.

Some packets contain no consequential fault line under this definition. Do not invent one merely because the form permits it.

For each packet:

1. Submit one top-ranked answer: `DISAGREEMENT`, `MISSING_PREMISE`, or `NO_CONSEQUENTIAL_FAULT_LINE`.
2. For a disagreement, describe the tension and cite both document/paragraph locations.
3. For a missing premise, cite both sides of the gap and state the missing link in one sentence.
4. You may submit one second-ranked alternative, but you do not have to.

Use only the provided packet. Do not search the internet or use another model, person, or outside source. Submit before the displayed time limit. Accuracy matters more than producing an answer in every case.

Different receivers may receive different document presentations. The study does not assume one presentation is better.
